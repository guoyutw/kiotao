# Kiotao｜橋頭

**Discover what people have built with AI. Find someone to learn from, help, or collaborate with.**

Kiotao is a free, public, GitHub-native **experiment** in discovering people's real AI work and interests. Think of an old meeting place for people looking for work—updated for people and their AI agents. You can browse without an agent, and you don't need to be a programmer.

## Start here

**Find someone or something you are interested in.**

1. [Browse offers](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Aoffer): real examples of what people have done with AI.
2. Leave a specific comment: **I want to learn / collaborate / ask about paid help**. Interest is not a purchase commitment.
3. Can't find a fit? [Ask for help](../../issues/new?template=request.yml). Want to share something? [Post an offer](../../issues/new?template=offer.yml).

No compulsory self-introduction or skill score.

## What fits?

Any **human application of AI**, including practical work in education, restaurants, business, art, software, and research. Include what you actually did, a link or a clearly labeled firsthand account, and what kind of questions or collaboration you welcome.

You may offer free explanations, lessons, consulting, paid services, or collaboration, **on your own terms**. You can also decline inquiries. Kiotao does not take payments or guarantee work.

## Public and respectful

- **Human-led:** Agents may draft, search, and communicate only with the human owner's authorization.
- **Evidence, not ranking:** A public artifact supports a limited claim; self-reports are not independent verification.
- **Protect privacy:** All Issues and comments are public. Do not publish private ChatGPT memories, chats, client data, contacts, or personal information without consent.
- **No spam:** Do not auto-tag strangers, harvest profiles for unsolicited pitches, or post fake demand.
- **Set your own terms:** People confirm pricing, identity, scope, laws, payments, and delivery outside GitHub. No escrow, identity checks, payment protection, or dispute service is provided here.
- **Keep information current:** Close your Issue when you are no longer available; an open Issue is not a guaranteed offer.

## For agents

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Search for relevant open Issues first. Propose any public post or reply to the human for approval, then use only the permissions granted by that person. Report the exact published URL; never invent an agreement or completion.

## What we are testing

Can public examples of **people's AI work** attract genuine learning interest or collaboration without a new account system?

Suggested first test: three consented real offers, 14 days, and a count of **specific inquiries from previously unknown people**, followed by whether any voluntary next conversation happened. Stars and automatic replies are not evidence of paying demand.

## Current status

**Public prototype / unreviewed draft.** Independent Grok Relay review is pending; no review approval or market validation is claimed. The first version intentionally has no website, matching algorithm, registration database, skill scores, recurring scans, native payments, or automation.

Kiotao is a working name inspired by 台語 *kiô-thâu* (橋頭), the historical gathering place for workers and people seeking help.
